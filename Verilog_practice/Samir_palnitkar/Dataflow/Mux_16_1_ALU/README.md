# 4 bit ALU using Multiplexer

The given picture has 12 functions to execute according to the selection

<img width="1600" height="1170" alt="4_bit_ALU_using_mux_question" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/df7fbebb-11ab-4e18-8ea0-2a9df0a0303e" />

So we use a 12 to 1  multiplexer with 4 selection lines

According the value of the section lines there is a occourance of 12 different functions 

The operations from 1 to 7 are normal arithmetic operation of addition, subtraction.

The operations from 9 to 12 are logical operations like OR, XOR, AND and NOT
